# Corporate website benchmark and design decisions

Reviewed 23 October 2026. These are ten selected strong corporate references relevant to Fortis, not a verified ranking of the world's ten best websites. Official homepage content was retrieved and browser appearance reviewed, subject to the limitations below. No competitor images, copy or code were reused.

| Reference | Useful observed pattern | Fortis adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Schneider Electric — https://www.se.com/ww/en/ | Clear solution hierarchy, green identity, prominent search and action routes | Four sector routes, search and consistent contact action |
| Siemens — https://www.siemens.com/global/en.html | Dark hero, concise headline, contrasting accent and paired actions | Deep green hero, gold emphasis and discover/partner actions |
| TOMRA — https://www.tomra.com/ | Bold circular transformation statement and direct solution navigation | Resource-to-value headline and circular bioeconomy spotlight |
| Veolia — https://www.veolia.com/en | Water, waste and energy pillars with featured stories | Connected sectors and a dedicated news/gallery route |
| Interface — https://www.interface.com/US/en-US.html | Bold type, warm neutral palette and category navigation | Editorial spacing, cream backgrounds and material-inspired colours |
| Novonesis — https://www.novonesis.com/en | Purpose-led introduction, large type and restrained green palette | Plain-language mission and accessible sector explanations |
| Ørsted — https://orsted.com/ | Retrieved content separates projects, resources, news and investors | Project pages, approved document library and funding context |
| Vestas — https://www.vestas.com/en | Spacious visual composition and slim navigation | Generous hero composition and uncluttered navigation |
| dsm-firmenich — https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/en/home.html | Large headline, rounded action and coherent business categories | Sector cards, stage badges and strong hierarchy |
| Unilever — https://www.unilever.com/ | Consistent business categories and editorial sections | Reusable content patterns for projects, updates and documents |

Limitations: Ørsted could not be visually inspected because the browser connection failed; its row is based on retrieved content only. Some hero media did not render on Interface, Vestas, dsm-firmenich and Unilever, so no conclusions were drawn about their video quality or performance. Schneider's region/cookie overlays obscured part of the initial view. This is a qualitative design review, not an accessibility or performance audit of those companies.

## Fortis-specific judgement

Fortis is developing projects, rather than presenting a mature multinational portfolio. The redesign therefore keeps current project stages visible and avoids invented operating results, testimonials, endorsements or partnerships. Original sector illustrations are labelled as concepts; real approved event photography can replace empty gallery states through the CMS. The supplied corporate logo remains central.

Heavy video, complex mega-menus, trackers and additional application backends were deliberately unnecessary for this small team's public website. The result keeps simple static hosting, locally hosted assets, batch publishing and a manageable editor.
